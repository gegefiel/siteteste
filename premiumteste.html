The why

Why those aches keep coming back

Persistent stiffness usually isn’t one big thing — it’s a small daily cycle. Understanding it is the first step to breaking it.

01

Blood flow slows down

When an area is stiff or overworked, local circulation drops. Less warmth and oxygen reach the tissue, and that tight, heavy feeling sets in.

02

Tissue tightens up

Muscles and connective tissue around the joint brace and shorten, which limits your comfortable range of movement.

03

The ache lingers

Without gentle heat and movement, the cycle repeats — so the same spots keep flaring up day after day.

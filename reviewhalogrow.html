How Grow Hairspray Works & How to Use It

To make sure the active ingredients sink in deeply, use the spray daily by following these simple directions:

  1. Apply directly to clean, dry or damp hair.
  2. Shake the container for a few seconds to mix the ingredients thoroughly.
  3. Create paths in your hair to focus the mist on your primary thinning zones.
  4. Hold the bottle 6-8 inches away and apply 8-10 sprays evenly.
  5. Use your fingertips to massage the liquid into your scalp for 30 seconds.
  6. Let it absorb for 2 minutes before styling your hair or using blow dryers.
